Implementation progress: 041 snowball and payoff dates

Phase 2 — domain
-----------------
Scope: design.md sections 1, 3, and 5 (snowball ordering, the two schedule payoff-date helpers, and maturity
removal), plus the minimal web changes needed to keep everything compiling and green.

What changed:

1. Snowball order (design.md 041 section 1). `PayoffScheduleCalculator.orderedLoans` now sorts debts by starting
   balance ascending (`Comparator.comparing(Debt::balance)`, a stable sort so ties keep the supplied order), then
   appends the mortgage last, unconditionally, exactly as before. The hand-verified cascade test was reworked with
   a debt that has a smaller balance but a lower rate than the other, so the new order provably differs from the
   old rate-descending order (see the corrected hand table in
   PayoffScheduleCalculatorTests.handVerifiedTwoDebtCaseMatchesTheFirstThreeMonthsExactly's comment). The ordering
   test and the "mortgage always last" test were reworked the same way (balance-based scenarios instead of
   rate-based ones).

2. Payoff-date helpers (design.md 041 section 3). `ScheduleLoan` gained a `boolean mortgage` component so
   `PayoffSchedule` can tell mortgage rows from debt rows without string-matching on the name "Mortgage".
   `PayoffSchedule` gained a `YearMonth asOf` component (so the payoff-date helpers have a month to report even
   with zero loans, when `months` is empty) and two instance methods:
     - `Optional<YearMonth> debtFreeMonth()`: the latest of every non-mortgage loan's closing month (the first
       month its `LoanMonth.end()` is zero), empty if any debt never closes within the schedule. With no debts at
       all (renter or mortgage-only), returns `Optional.of(asOf)` — documented on the method and the record's
       `asOf` component as the chosen representation of the "trivially already debt-free" case.
     - `Optional<YearMonth> mortgagePayoffMonth()`: the mortgage row's own closing month, empty if there is no
       mortgage row or it never closes within the schedule.
   Both are computed from the existing `loans`/`months` data (no additional per-month bookkeeping needed).

3. Maturity removal (design.md 041 section 1). `Mortgage` no longer has a `maturity` component (now a 3-arg
   record: balance, annualInterestRate, monthlyPayment). `MortgagePayoff` dropped `maturityComparison` and
   `monthsDifference`; `MortgagePayoff.MaturityComparison` is gone. `MortgagePayoffCalculator.payoff` no longer
   takes or compares against a maturity — it just returns `principalAndInterest`, `firstMonthInterest`, `covered`,
   `monthsToPayoff` (null if not covered or capped), and `payoffMonth`. `MortgageEvaluator`'s blocker is now only
   the current-payment sentence, per design.md 041 section 2:
     - covered and finishes: "At your current payment it's paid off in {Month YYYY}."
     - not covered: "Your $P payment doesn't cover the $I monthly interest." (unchanged wording)
     - capped: "At your current payment it won't be paid off within 50 years." (new wording, was "This payment
       won't pay the loan off within 50 years.")
   The duration/maturity-comparison helpers (word/duration/monthYear-follow-up) were deleted from
   MortgageEvaluator; only `monthYear` remains, for the payoff-line sentence.

Web stopgaps (minimal, compile-keeping only; full copy/ResultViews/templates work is phase 3):
  - `SnapshotFormMapper` no longer reads `maturityMonth`/`maturityYear` from the form at all (the mapper's
    `maturity(...)`/`parseInt(...)` helpers and the maturity range constants were deleted). `SnapshotForm`'s
    maturity fields and `form.html`'s maturity inputs are untouched — they still round-trip through
    `/edit`/`/debts/add` etc. as plain hidden-field echoes, just no longer consumed into the domain.
  - `SampleHouseholds.debtFree()`/`homestretch()` no longer compute or set a maturity (the `matchingMaturity`/
    `setMaturity`/`HOMESTRETCH_AHEAD_MONTHS` machinery was deleted); `byId(id, asOf)` keeps its `asOf` parameter
    for the caller's (SnapshotController's) sake, but no sample branch uses it anymore.
  - `ResultViews.stage6VerdictSub` was not touched — it already only used `covered`/`monthsToPayoff`/
    `payoffMonth`, none of which moved.
  - `ScheduleViews` was not touched — it doesn't read `ScheduleLoan.mortgage()` or the new schedule helpers.

  - `SnapshotControllerTests`: the two sample-maturity assertions ("value=\"2044\"...", "value=\"2038\"...") on
