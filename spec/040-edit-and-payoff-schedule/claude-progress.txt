2. New package `domain.projection`: PayoffScheduleCalculator
   - Records: ScheduleLoan(name, startingBalance, annualRate, minimumPayment), LoanMonth(open, interest,
     payment, end), ScheduleMonth(month, extraCash, cascade, totalAvailable, loanMonths, monthInterest,
     totalBalance, cumulativeInterest), PayoffSchedule(loans, months, totalExtraCash, totalPaidPerLoan,
     totalInterest, truncated).
   - PayoffScheduleCalculator.schedule(FinancialSnapshot): debts sorted by rate descending (stable sort keeps
     supplied order on ties), mortgage appended last only when its balance is above $0 (name "Mortgage",
     minimum = its monthly payment). extraCash = max(monthlySurplus, $0), constant every month. Each month:
     cascade = sum of minimums of loans closed by an earlier month (freed minimum counted once, the month
     after it closes, not the closing month itself); totalAvailable = extraCash + cascade; for each open loan
     in order, interest = open * rate/1200 (DECIMAL128 monthly rate, Money.times half-even cents, same
     pattern as MortgagePayoffCalculator), payment = min(open+interest, minimum+pool), pool reduced by
     max(payment-minimum, 0) after each loan; end = open+interest-payment (always >= 0 since payment is
     capped at open+interest). Closed loans render as an all-$0 LoanMonth. Stops the month the last loan's
     balance reaches $0, or at 600 months (`truncated=true`); any ArithmeticException (overflow) part-way
     through a month is caught and also treated as truncated, per the task's "must not throw" requirement,
     rather than propagating.
   - No loans (renter with no debts, or a paid-off mortgage and no debts) returns an empty schedule with all
     totals at $0 and `truncated=false`, without entering the simulation loop.
